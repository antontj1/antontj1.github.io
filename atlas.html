2025–2026

Student-led multi-band lens modeling

INAGLENS students modeled 60 SuGOHI systems in HSC grizy imaging with lenstronomy, using an EPL plus external shear for mass and Sérsic models for light.

Completed modeling
2026 · ongoing

Population-level IMF and halo analysis

Analysis is underway to infer the IMF mismatch parameter and halo-mass distribution, using weak-lensing halo-mass priors and accounting for strong-lens selection effects.

Work in progress

CosmoLens · Institut Teknologi Bandung

ITB’s research group develops strong-lensing discovery and mass modeling, dark-matter studies, and student research. The group anchors the domestic research activity represented in this atlas.

ITB students and INAGLENS training

Students based in Indonesia have taken part in lensing training and modeled 60 SuGOHI systems. INAGLENS itself is an international network, described in the international column.

Observatory · education · community

Timau · ITB, BRIN, Universitas Nusa Cendana, and local partners

Joint work on development and use of the Timau National Observatory, astronomy education, and community engagement. In October 2024, an ITB–Undana–BRIN team led by Anton Timur Jaelani and Prananda L. Malasan held a workshop in Amfoang Tengah to map cultural and natural assets around the observatory.

Promising collaboration pathways

These are possibilities to discuss and develop; the map distinguishes them from established research and mentoring links.

01 / JOINT SCIENCE

Lens mass, IMF & dark matter

Build on the 60 student-modeled SuGOHI lenses to compare stellar and total mass, then develop joint population analyses and follow-up studies.

ITB · RESCEU · UCHICAGO · TOHOKU
02 / COMPUTE & METHODS

Scale up modeling with HPC

Explore supercomputing workflows for multi-band lens modeling, uncertainty analysis, and machine-learning-assisted candidate studies.

ITB · TSUKUBA · TOHOKU
03 / PEOPLE & TRAINING

Joint supervision & exchange

Connect student projects, short research visits, and possible double-degree pathways so students can contribute to shared lensing work.

ITB · KINDAI · TSUKUBA · TOHOKU
04 / OBSERVING CAPACITY

Timau–Seimei knowledge exchange

Share experience in telescope operations and scientific planning to support the development of observing programs at Timau.

ITB · KYOTO · TIMAU
05 / MACHINE LEARNING

ML for lens discovery & inference

Connect student-led ML work with physical lens models, combining candidate detection with interpretable parameter estimation.

ITB · KINDAI · UCHICAGO · INAGLENS
06 / SHARED DATA

HSC lens sample development

Strengthen shared workflows for image preparation, model quality checks, and consistent measurements across the SuGOHI sample.
